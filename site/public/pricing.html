Pricing questions

Something else? Email our team.

Do I need a card to start?

Yes. It keeps the trial to real agencies. You're not charged for 14 days, and you can cancel before then and pay nothing.

How do I pay?

Apple Pay, Google Pay, or any major card, through Stripe's secure checkout. Wellby never sees or stores your card number.

What counts as a user?

Anyone on your team who signs in, such as marketers, managers and owners. Add or remove users anytime from Settings → Billing, and billing adjusts automatically.

Can I switch plans?

Yes, anytime, from Settings → Billing in the app.

What happens after the trial?

Your card is charged for your plan on day 15. Before then, you'll get a reminder email.

Do you offer annual billing?

Yes, and it includes 2 months free.

Do you have a founding agency offer?

Yes. The first 20 agencies get 40% off for life in return for a short case study. Contact us and we'll send you a promo code.

Are there setup fees or contracts?

No setup fees. No contracts. Pay monthly or yearly, and cancel anytime.
