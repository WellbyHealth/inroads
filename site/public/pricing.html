FAQ

Pricing questions

Something else? Email our team.

Which plan is right for me?

Solo is for one person doing the referral work. Team is for 2 to 10 people who share accounts and territories. Enterprise is for 10 or more users, or several locations, and adds rep scorecards, goals and performance insights for owners and managers.

Do I need a card to start?

Yes, for Solo and Team. It keeps the trial to real agencies. You're not charged for 14 days, and you can cancel before then and pay nothing.

How do I pay?

Apple Pay, Google Pay, or any major card, through Stripe's secure checkout. Wellby never sees or stores your card number.

What counts as a user?

Anyone on your team who signs in, such as marketers, managers and owners. Solo includes 1 user. Team includes 3, and you can add more for $79 a month each, up to 10. Billing adjusts automatically.

Can I switch plans?

Yes, anytime, from Settings → Billing in the app. To move to Enterprise, email us and we'll set it up.

How is Enterprise priced?

It depends on your number of users and locations, starting at 10 users. Book a short demo and we'll send a quote. Enterprise is billed annually.

What happens after the trial?

Your card is charged for your plan on day 15. Before then, you'll get a reminder email.

Do you offer annual billing?

Yes, and it includes 2 months free on Solo and Team.

Do you have a founding agency offer?

Yes. The first 20 agencies on Solo or Team get 40% off for life in return for a short case study. Contact us and we'll send you a promo code.

Are there setup fees or contracts?

No setup fees. Solo and Team have no contracts: pay monthly or yearly, and cancel anytime. Enterprise is an annual agreement.
