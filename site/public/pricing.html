FAQ

Pricing questions

Why aren't prices listed yet?

We're finalizing plans now and will post prices here as soon as they're set. Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial, and you can sign up without a sales call.

How will pricing work?

You add a card when you start your trial, and the first charge comes when the 14 days end. Cancel before then and you pay nothing. We'll post the details here when plans are final.

Can we start small and move up?

That's the idea. Many providers will start on Insight to see their referral picture, then move to Growth when they want agents doing the outreach.

Do agents send messages without approval?

No. On every plan, a marketer approves each message by default, and it goes out from their own email.

What about HIPAA?

On every plan, we'll sign a business associate agreement before any provider connects real patient data.
