The problem

Your referral intelligence is scattered everywhere.

  • Referrals arrive as emails, face sheets and phone calls. Your care software can't tell you which partner sent the most this quarter.

  • Each marketer carries a personal network, and there are far more hospitals, rehabs and discharge planners worth seeing than people to see them.

  • A partner says they've been sending you business. Without the numbers, you can't say otherwise.

  • You send a nursing facility patients for rehab. They're supposed to send some back. Nobody is keeping count.

  • Market-data tools hand you dashboards built on claims. Private-pay clients barely show up, and your team still has to write and chase every message.

FAQ

Common questions

Still curious? Ask our team or see pricing.

Is this a CRM?

No. A CRM waits for someone to type in every call and visit. Wellby is referral intelligence plus a system of action: it's built to work from the referrals you already receive, show which relationships drive growth, and do the follow-up work your team doesn't have time for. If you use a CRM, Wellby sits alongside it.

Who is it for?

Owners and growth teams (marketers, community liaisons, business development and admissions outreach) at care providers that grow through referrals. We start with post-acute care (home health, hospice, skilled nursing, rehab and home care) and extend to home infusion, senior living, behavioral health and addiction treatment. The referral sources differ, from discharge planners to therapists, psychiatrists and emergency departments, but the work is the same.

How is this different from Trella or Definitive Healthcare?

They give you data: claims-based market views, dashboards and, with Trella, a CRM to log the work. Your team still decides who to call, writes every message and chases every reply. Wellby is built to do that work: agents that rank who to contact, draft the outreach in your team's voice and follow up until partners reply, using your own referrals, private pay included. See Wellby vs Trella and Wellby vs Definitive.

Does Wellby send messages for us?

It drafts them and queues them by priority. By default a marketer approves each one, and it goes out from their own email so replies come back to them. Wellby then watches the thread and drafts the follow-up if no one replies.

How much work is setup?

Very little for your team. Sign up in the browser, add your agency name, website and offices, and Wellby maps the referral sources around each office. Bring your referral history in with a CSV upload. There's no sales call and no new forms for intake to fill in.

Will my marketers actually use it?

It's built around how they already work: a short weekly plan and check-ins drafted for them to review and send from their own email. There's nothing to log after a visit unless they want to add a note.

Does it work across multiple locations?

That's the plan. Each location gets its own scorecard and visit plan, with a roll-up for owners who want to compare locations and territories.

Which systems will it connect to?

Today you bring referrals in with a CSV upload. Next we're building connections to AxisCare and HubSpot, then Gmail and Outlook. None of those connections are live yet. Tell us what you use and it helps us decide what comes next.

What about HIPAA?

Referrals contain patient information, so we're designing Wellby around HIPAA requirements from the start and will sign a business associate agreement before any provider connects real data. We aren't claiming compliance for a product that isn't live yet.

What will it cost?

Solo is $199 a month for one user and one location. Team is $499 a month for one location and up to 5 users, plus $149 for each extra location, which adds 3 users. Enterprise is priced after a short demo, for groups with 6 or more locations. Annual billing includes 2 months free, and Solo and Team start with a 14-day free trial. You add a card at sign-up and aren't charged until the trial ends. See pricing for details.

When can we start?

Wellby is in development, with self-serve sign-up rolling out now. When it's open, you'll start a free trial and be working within minutes.

What's on the roadmap?

Next up is lead tracking: following up with people who asked about care but haven't started, and helping your team turn them into clients, with agents doing the follow-up. It isn't built yet.
