Trella gives you data. Wellby does the work.

Trella is built around Medicare claims data and a CRM where reps plan and log their outreach. That tells you who to call. Wellby is built to make the call happen: agents that decide who to contact today, write the message and follow up until the partner replies.

With Trella, your team still

  • Turns claims dashboards into a call list by hand
  • Writes every thank-you, check-in and update
  • Logs visits and notes in the CRM
  • Chases each partner who doesn't reply
  • Works without private-pay and community referrals that claims data doesn't capture

With Wellby, agents are built to

  • Rank who to contact today, from market data and your own referrals
  • Draft every message in your team's voice, ready to approve
  • Send it from your marketer's own email and follow up until there's a reply
  • Count every referral, including private pay, with no data entry
  • Flag partners going quiet before the quarter is lost

What Trella customers report

In Trella's own case studies, customers credit it with focus and credibility. One hospice narrowed each rep's list from 30 to 40 accounts down to 14 to 20, and one post-acute provider saw one physician go from sending it 50% of their referrals to 80% after a data-backed visit. Of 25 published case studies, only a handful report a number, and few give a baseline or timeframe. Wellby is built to close that loop, measuring which outreach actually won referrals.

Already using Trella?

Keep the data if your team relies on it. Wellby is built to work from the referrals you already receive and do the outreach and follow-up a CRM leaves to your reps.
