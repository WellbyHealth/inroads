Definitive gives you data. Wellby does the work.

Definitive sells healthcare data, mostly to health systems, life sciences and medtech. Great for looking things up. Wellby is built for referral-driven providers: agents turn the data into outreach and follow up.

With Definitive, your team still

  • Searches provider, affiliation and claims data
  • Exports lists into a CRM
  • Turns dashboards into a weekly plan by hand
  • Writes and sends every message
  • Has no view of the private-pay referrals you actually receive

With Wellby, agents are built to

  • Rank who to contact today, from market data and your own referrals
  • Draft every thank-you, check-in and update for approval
  • Follow up until the partner replies, then book the visit
  • Track every source month over month and quarter over quarter
  • Work for small teams with no analysts
